For contractors

The job-management system that runs without you

For owners of small civil and construction businesses who are neglecting the work to babysit the admin.

  • Quotes go out the same day, from a template the crew can fill in.
  • Every variation is written down, priced and approved before the work is done.
  • Invoices go out the day the job closes, not the end of the month.
  • Timesheets, dockets and subbie paperwork land in one place, from the foreman's phone.
  • The owner stops being the person everyone rings to find out where things are.
Built in the tools you already run where they work. A contractor already on Xero keeps Xero. Every deliverable works on the foreman's phone, or it won't survive a fortnight.
For appointment businesses

An AI phone receptionist that books straight into your calendar

For salons, clinics and studios that miss calls when every chair is busy, and lose the booking to the next name on Google.

  • Answers every call, first ring, seven days, after hours included.
  • Books the right service, the right staff member and the right length of slot into your existing booking system.
  • Recognises regulars by their number and sees their history before it says hello.
  • Texts the client a confirmation and a deposit link, and texts you anything it couldn't handle.
  • Hands the call to a person the moment it should.
Runs on your existing number. Set up in days, tuned over a fortnight, and I come back to fix what it got wrong. It takes the calls nobody can get to; it doesn't replace your front desk.

What it does on a call

It books the way your best receptionist would.

Every call answered, every booking right

  • Answers first ring, seven days, after hours included.
  • Checks real availability and only offers times that are actually free.
  • Books the right service, the right person and the right length of slot. It knows who works which days and who does what.
  • Recognises regulars by their number and books them with their usual person.
  • Reads the booking back and only books it once the caller says yes.

Your rules, not a script

  • Follows the way you run the business. For example: new colour clients get a consultation and patch test first, consultations are for new clients only, and long appointments need a deposit.
  • Texts the client a confirmation, plus a deposit link when you want one. The slot is held until it's paid.
  • Hands the call to a person, or takes a message and texts it to you, the moment it should. Complaints come straight to you.
It takes the calls nobody can get to. It doesn't replace your front desk.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

What does an AI receptionist cost?

A fixed setup fee and a flat monthly fee. Your scope is assessed before you commit, so you know the full price up front. No lock-in.

Does it work with my booking system?

It books into the system you already run, where that system allows an outside connection. I check that before you commit. If yours doesn't allow it, I tell you straight and we look at the options.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. It runs on your existing number. You choose when calls go to it: when the desk is busy, after hours, or every call.

Will callers know it's not a person?

It speaks naturally, in an Australian voice. If someone asks, it tells them straight: it's the business's AI receptionist, sometimes called a virtual receptionist. That's software that answers the phone, checks your diary and makes bookings the way your front desk would. Then it offers to put them through to a person.

What happens when it can't help?

It hands the call to a person, or takes a message and texts it to you, the moment it should. Complaints and anything unusual go straight to you.

Do you work outside Sydney?

Yes. It's set up and tuned remotely, anywhere in Australia, and I'm on site when it helps.

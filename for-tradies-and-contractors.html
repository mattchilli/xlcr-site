AI receptionist for tradies

  • Answers first ring, seven days, after hours included.
  • Takes the job details properly: name, address, what the job is and how urgent.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

The admin system is a fixed price, quoted after a discovery call. The AI receptionist is a fixed setup fee and a flat monthly fee. Your scope is assessed before you commit, so you know the full price up front. No lock-in.

Do I have to change software?

Not if what you've got works. I build in the tools you already run where they do the job. A contractor already on Xero keeps Xero. I only replace what's broken.

Will customers know they're talking to AI?

It speaks naturally, in an Australian voice. If someone asks, it tells them straight: it's the business's AI receptionist, sometimes called a virtual receptionist. That's software that answers the phone, takes the job details and books the visit the way your office would. Then it offers to put them through to a person.

Does it replace my office person?

No. It takes the calls nobody can get to, and the system takes the chasing off their plate. Your office gets time back for the work that needs a person.

Do you come on site?

Yes. The first day is on site, on the tools or shadowing you, watching how the work actually flows. Sydney based, Australia wide, and I travel for the right job.

Can you write my safety documents?

No. I build the register and the process. I'm not a WHS consultant, a lawyer or an accountant. Where you need one, the deliverable says so and tells you what to ask them.
